Digital Divide down Under
2014

Understanding Digital Inclusion in Australia

We are living in a digital era where internet access is essential for education, work, and daily life. Yet not all Australians are equally included. This project explores how household income and wealth affect digital inclusion, highlighting inequalities in access, affordability, and digital ability across states, territories, and income groups.

Australian Digital Inclusion Map

Digital Inclusion Across States

This choropleth map shows the Digital Inclusion Index by state and territory. Higher values indicate better access, affordability, and digital skills. The Australian Capital Territory consistently scores highest, while Northern Territory and Tasmania score lowest. Most regions show gradual improvement over time, but gaps remain, highlighting ongoing digital inequalities.

Digital Inclusion Across Income Groups

This heatmap illustrates the state of digital inclusion in Australia over seven years, focusing on three key dimensions: Access, Affordability, and Digital Ability. Scores are broken down by income quintiles (Q1–Q5), where Q1 represents the lowest-income households and Q5 the highest. Access measures availability of internet and devices, Affordability reflects the cost of digital services relative to income, and Digital Ability captures digital skills and confidence.

Digital Access Disparities by Income Quintile

Income Distribution per week by Quintile

This treemap visualizes how total income is distributed across different
income quintiles per week. Each rectangle represents a quintile, and
the size of the rectangle corresponds to the proportion of
total income earned by that group. It allows for easy comparison
between lower and higher income groups.


Composition of Main Income Sources

This doughnut chart shows the breakdown of households’ main sources
of income, such as employee income or government transfer.
Each slice represents the relative share of households relying on
that income source, making it simple to see which sources
are most prevalent.

Comparing Digital Inclusion Across States

This grouped bar chart highlights how each Australian state performs
across key digital inclusion metrics, such as Access, Affordability,
and Digital Ability. It allows a direct comparison of strengths
and weaknesses between states, revealing areas where certain regions
excel and others lag behind. For clarity, annotations are only shown
for Victoria, highlighting key scores in that state.


Australia’s Digital Inclusion Profile

This radar chart presents Australia’s overall performance across the
three key dimensions of digital inclusion: Access, Affordability,
and Digital Ability. It provides a clear snapshot of strengths
and areas for improvement at the national level, highlighting how
well Australians are connected, able to afford digital services, and
equipped with the skills to participate in the digital world.
